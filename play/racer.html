Off-road Racer

Race through every checkpoint as fast as you can across unknown terrain. You only see what your sensors can see: hills and tall vegetation block your view, so ridges hide what lies beyond. Trails are fast, vegetation and creeks are slow, and hitting a ditch at speed hurts.
