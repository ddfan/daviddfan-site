Off-road Racer

Reach all six checkpoints as fast as you can across unmapped terrain. Your sensors only see what the land lets them: hills and thick vegetation hide what is beyond. The yellow arrow points at the next checkpoint, but the straight line is rarely the quickest way.

↑/W gas · ↓/S brake and reverse · ←→/AD steer · Space handbrake · R restart.

Trails give you a speed boost, but they are not always there: a good road can narrow to a game trail and disappear into the brush, then reappear further on. Creeks and ditches are very slow to cross and can leave you stuck, so hit them slowly and reverse out if you bog down. Forest and brush slow you, uphill slows you, and hills block your view.
