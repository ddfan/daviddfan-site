Tube Dodge

Steer your vehicle with the mouse, or touch and drag. The green tube shows where you might end up: it widens the faster you go, because actuation noise grows with speed. Keep obstacles out of the tube for bonus points; touching one costs a life.

Click the field to start.

Controls: move the mouse (the vehicle accelerates toward your pointer) or use ←↑↓→. Obstacles get more numerous over time.
