Mine Explorer

Lead a fleet of four robots into an unmapped mine. Select a robot, click where you want it to go, and it finds its own way there, dropping comms relays behind it. Discoveries only reach the base while a robot is inside comms range, and a robot that runs out of battery is gone for good.

How to play

base / relaycomms rangereported artifactno link

Click a robot (or press 1–4) to select it, then click the map to send it to that area. It plans through unexplored space and replans when it hits rock.

Robots drop relays automatically near the edge of comms range. Anything a robot sees outside comms is only uploaded when it comes back into range, so a robot that dies out there takes its discoveries with it.

Score: 100 per reported artifact, 25 per surviving robot, plus the percent of the mine mapped. Space pauses, R recalls the selected robot.
