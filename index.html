David D. Fan

I'm David Fan, CTO of Field AI, where I lead the team building Field Foundation Models: autonomy that lets robots work in the real world without maps, GPS or predefined paths.

I've spent my career building robots that leave the lab. Before Field AI, I was a research engineer at the Navy's NIWC and a research fellow at NASA JPL, where I served as chief technologist and chief engineer on DARPA's SubT Challenge and RACER programs.

Work

2022 – present

Field AI

As CTO, I lead the team building Field Foundation Models, a new kind of AI brain that gives robots real-world risk awareness. It runs across many kinds of robots and is already at work on industrial sites around the world. We're hiring.

DARPA RACER · 2021 – 2022

Fast driving, off the road

I was chief technologist for JPL's RACER effort and PI for NIWC's, working with partners at Georgia Tech, MIT and Berkeley. We taught vehicles to read rough terrain and drive fast through it, using learned traversability and risk-aware planning. IEEE Spectrum coverage

DARPA Subterranean Challenge · NASA JPL

Team CoSTAR

I was part of JPL's Team CoSTAR, winner of Phase II. Our legged and wheeled robots explored underground tunnels, mines and caves entirely on their own.

PhD · Georgia Tech

Safe robot planning and control

My thesis, Safe Robot Planning and Control Using Uncertainty-Aware Deep Learning, combined machine learning with classical control and planning so robots can be both capable and safe. It covers tube MPC, Bayesian adaptive control and risk-aware costmaps. Advised by Evangelos Theodorou.
